# Moonlit: Free vs Premium legal search

_Researched 2026-10-09_

**TL;DR:** Mostly yes. Moonlit says the free plan searches the same full Data Layer as Premium. The paid plans add more AI usage and more tools, not a better search index. No independent source confirms or contradicts this.

## What's the same

From [moonlit.ai/pricing](https://www.moonlit.ai/pricing):

- Every plan, including Free, has "Search across the full Data Layer".
- The comparison table lists "every jurisdiction" and "source tracing on every citation" as included, without splitting them by plan.

## What Premium adds

Premium costs €112.50/month excl. VAT.

| | Free | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Luna (AI assistant) chats | 5 a week | Unlimited |
| Fast answers | Not listed | Unlimited |
| Deep research | Not listed | 30 a month |
| Workspaces | No | Yes, 1,000 answers a month |
| Monitors (alerts) | No | Yes, 1,000 answers a month |
| MCP calls | 100 a month | 100 a month |

There's also a Student plan at €6.26/month with unlimited chat, 5 deep research runs and Workspaces.

## Caveats

- The pricing page doesn't say which AI model each plan uses. Free and Premium might run different models or different depths of answers, and nothing published says either way.
- The page doesn't mention search filters or result limits per plan.
- The pricing page contradicts itself: the comparison table shows €125/€79/€39, while the plan cards show €112.50/€71.10/€35.10.
- I found no independent reviews comparing the tiers. The G2 page for "Moonlit Platform" is a different product, a no-code automation tool.

## Next step

To settle it, run the same query on a free and a Premium account and compare the sources and Luna's answers, or ask Moonlit directly whether Luna uses the same model on every plan.
